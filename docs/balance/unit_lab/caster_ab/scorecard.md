# Unit Lab: caster_ab

1600 fights. Score = side A's rate (win 1, draw 0.5) with a Wilson 95 % interval. Teams alternate per seed (odd seeds: A = player team, even: A = enemy team). Generated by tools/unit_lab/unit_lab_report.gd.

> Each caster inside its kingdom's line unit (1 caster at full mana + the line) against the SAME line unit alone, at equal crystals (~1080) and at equal pop (12): does adding the caster beat adding the same crystals / supply of line units? Side A = the caster army; A score > 50 % = the caster pays. Rimecarver runs the real AICasters ('bot': its spells are autocast off). Use an even number of seeds (team alternation).
