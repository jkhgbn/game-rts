# Blitz pace (2026-10-10)

JS 2026-10-10: a BLITZ mode that starts with 9 workers, where games last 8 to 12 minutes. It is a third choice on the
skirmish setup's Pace row, **Normal / Fast (test) / Blitz**. The default stays Normal. The choice is saved in the
settings (`GameSettings.pace_blitz`) and in save games (`Skirmish.blitz_pace`, in `SaveGame.LEVEL_PROPS`). Missions
never use it.

## Verdict
- **Length: met.** The median is 10:57 at normal and 11:25 at hard (real time). 0-3 % of games end before 5:00, and the
  first attacker wins 53-58 % of games.
- **Kingdom balance: not met.** Pooled over 36 games per level, Fire wins 61 % / 72 % (normal / hard), Shadow 28 / 61,
  **Ice 28 / 22** and Nature 83 / 44. Ice is below 35 % in every arm. Its start bank (+800) and its training factor
  (x0.35) did not move it. This matches the 10-08 finding that the Ice bot does not turn extra income into army, so it
  is a Commander issue (ai-coder), not a data issue. On Thornwater, Fire won 83-100 % of normal games in every arm (12
  batches of 6 Fire games each). The Fire wizard kills the most workers (4.7 per game against 1.2 for Ice). With 9
  workers in a line, AoE spells hit a lot of them. Doubling worker HP was the lever that helped most (Fire 89 -> 61 %
  in the v11 -> v12 pair).
- Samples are small (6-9 games per kingdom per map). A difference of 20 points between two arms is within noise.

## Final values (`scripts/world/pace_switch.gd` `PaceSwitch.BLITZ`; bot knobs changed in pass 2 below)

| Soldier train time | x0.5 (Fire x0.9, Ice x0.35) | x0.7 |

| Keep tiers (`TierSystem.pace_time_x`) | x0.5: Stronghold from 1:45 (60 s -> 30 s), Citadel from 2:53 (45 s), bot timeline x0.5 | - |
| Game clock (`Engine.time_scale`) | **x1.3** (restored when the level leaves) | - |

The clock multiplier is needed. Without it (v1-v4), data alone reached 13-15 min of game time. The winner beats the first
army at about 3-5 min, then needs about 10 more minutes to kill a base that keeps training soldiers.

## Kingdom balance pass 2 (2026-10-10, later)
Goal: each kingdom 35-65 % at normal / hard, median 8-12 min, < 5 % before 5:00, first attacker < ~70 %.
Arms `blitz_v16` .. `blitz_v24`, normal, Crown Hollow (CH) + Thornwater (TW), 2 seeds each, all 12 ordered pairs
(48 games per arm, 24 per map). Runs `fg-bz9` .. `fg-bz14tw`.

**Findings**
- **Ice did build an army.** In bz8 (v15) Ice had the biggest army at 3:00 (15 pop, others 8-12) and the most
  buildings, but made only 2 attacks per game. The bot needed ~12 pop at home before it could leave (min_attack_pop 8
  plus a 2-soldier home reserve, often a pop-3 Rime Colossus). Blitz-only Ice knobs (new BLITZ key `ai_kingdom`):
  min_attack_pop 6, attack_ratio / floor x0.8, home reserve 1 (new AIProfile knob `attack_reserve`, default 2). Ice then
  attacks 4.2 times per game, first at 2:27 (was 2:54).
- **The defender wins, not Fire's spells.** In v16-v19 the first attacker won only 17-42 %. The other bots attack at
  ~2:50 with ~16 pop, Fire's smaller army (8 pop) holds at home, and the attack falls apart. Halving spell damage on
  workers (new key `worker_spell_x`) and cutting Fire's spell damage to x0.7 / x0.6 (new key `spell_damage_x`) did not
  move Fire on TW (83-92 %). Fire's opponents lose about as many workers on TW as on CH (15 vs 17 per game), so the
  worker line layout is not what makes the difference. Slower Fire training (v15 x0.9) makes Fire play defence even more.
- **Calmer bots fix Crown Hollow.** Bot attack_ratio / floor x0.9 instead of x0.75 (v20): CH 58/50/42/50 (spread 17,
  was 42/33/33/92 in v15).
- **Thornwater still favours Fire** in every arm (83-100 %). The first attacker wins 12-33 % of TW games vs 38-54 % on
  CH. That needs a map or Fire-defence look, not a Blitz number.
