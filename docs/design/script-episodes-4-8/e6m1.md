## 2. Opening and between-scene beats
**`ep6_opening` "Patience and Heart"** (outline 3.6 and D1: "0:30 recap (a different cut), then Torvald's and Ysolde's
big intros"; ER8: the winter truce frame). 0:00-0:32, the recap: five prologue shots re-cut with their own rendered lines,
chosen to differ from `ep5_opening`'s (which uses S01, S02, S06, S07, S12, S30): the peace (S05, the kings), the
vanishing (S07, the only shared line: it is this episode's subject), Torvald's and Ysolde's own prologue lines (S22,
S26), and "I know this tale well, child. I was that wizard." (S13) handing to the memories. 0:32-1:45, Torvald (first person, present-day frame on the broken Wall with Aldra, the `ep4_ending` W6 image): new
origin (a boy keeping a beacon all winter on a mountain pass for soldiers who never came back; the Master comes up the
road in spring: "I need someone who keeps a light"), strength (the north, the lake, the block of ice with a light inside,
a hundred winters), weakness (prologue S24 from the rider's side, left open: "I stood there counting", the e6m2 choice is
not told), love ("He called me his patience. Twelve years now, I have waited"). 1:45-3:00, Ysolde (first person, Lark
Hill in snow with Maelis): her childhood is NOT retold (`ep4_opening` L2-L6 told it): one line points at it ("That part
is easy to tell") and the block shows what came after: twenty years of dead things brought back to life ("Show me
again."), the west given to her (army shot), the weakness (prologue S28: she closed her roads during the Night of
Knives), the love (the green flame in her palm, "the part that keeps going when the rest stops"), and the hook for e6m4
and the ending: "I promised not to. So did someone else." Title card; into e6m1's briefing.

**`c_e6_lake`** (spec beats): present day, the winter truce; Torvald and Aldra (fifties) at a fire on the same shore,
M2's hole in the ice frozen over; the spec's three lines ("A hundred winters I kept it for him." / "And a girl with a
fire took it in an afternoon." / "She did. I have worse things to remember."), opened by Aldra ("The ice has stopped
singing", the e6m1 L5 echo) and closed on "The gate." (into e6m2).
