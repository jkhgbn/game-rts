## 2. Episode ending: `ep7_ending` "No. Not for Now."
Spec beats: dawn on the stilled Coinwater, the whole strait glass. Torvald walks out across the ice to Malrik's frozen
galley, Aldra a step behind. Malrik, the stone dim in his hand: "So. No." Torvald: "No. Not for now. Not ever." (the title;
the answer to ep1 C1's "No. For now."). Malrik smiles thinly: "We will see who waits longer, Torvald." and steps down into
the shadow of his hull and is gone. Dacian and Seraphine sit on the rim beside the empty chair, sharing water from one
flask (`ep8_ending` E21's image, younger). Ossavar lifts off the chair; voice-over: "Gold for fire, they say. He paid in
neither." He flies north, toward the Master's empty sky (pro. S30). Last image: Torvald and Aldra on the ice, small, the
sun coming up. Title `Episode 7 · Gold for Fire · End`. The Reward section adds the ring (`ossavar_ring`, "The one price I
ever gave back"), given to Torvald in this film.
