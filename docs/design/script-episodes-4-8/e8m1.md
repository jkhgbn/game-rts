## 2. Episode opening: `ep8_opening` "The Four Keys"
Outline 4.1 beats: spring after the Coinwater; every battle of the war has run down into the Hollow (ep1: "the war itself
is opening the door"); in Malrik's hand the heart-stone cracks and speaks in a full voice; under Crown Hollow the
Master's light flickers; at Emberfall Old Vey: "He can't hold it. Not alone. Not any more."; Vessa does the one thing
nobody expects from her and gives the relics back (the Orb to Torvald, it lay in his lake; the Signet to Ysolde, it
slept in her tree; the Ember Crown stays hers); "Three keys." "The fourth is in Malrik's pocket."; Vey: "Then we go and
ask him. He asked for all four of you. Especially Malrik."; title; the Story menu asks "Whose road do you walk?". The
spec adds (A.1, C.1, D.1, 9 note 7): one morning, two relics given away; the Orb travels to Torvald with the message
"We need all four of you."; Ysolde gets the Signet back at home; the StoryFlow step moves the two relics between the
tracks (`ep8_relics_returned`). Every briefing of the four roads ends on `Four keys. One door. Yours is on this road.`
(1.1, briefing text, not a film line).
