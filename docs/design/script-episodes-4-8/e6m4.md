yourself. I knew you would."); the Master: "Then we will both learn patience, Obren. I have had a good teacher." (the
Torvald echo); he pulls the crack shut behind him; the prologue's own line plays as the seam goes dark; above, from the
floor, the robe falls and the regalia burst through the dome; the four streaks leave the citadel and every dragon turns;
narrator: "Two of the four knew where he had gone. For twelve years, they told no one." (Ep1's silence, e6m4 L17); the
blue and green streaks part north and west; title card.
