## 1. High: e8m3 soft-lock when the King falls while the defeat is pending

- `scripts/campaign/mission_e8_03.gd:318` `_on_king_hit` (a `damage_taken` handler), `:175` the King's `death_guard`
  (calls `_check_fall`), `:343` `_check_fall`, `:640` `_fall`, `:652` `hold_world(true)`.
- What is wrong: none of `_on_king_hit`, `_check_fall`, `_fall`, `_phase2`, `_phase3` checks `is_ending()`. They run
  from a signal / death guard, so the physics tick's `is_ending()` return does not protect them.
- Scenario: phase 3, an Exposed window, the King near 1 HP, `turns >= TURNS_MIN`. The player's wizard dies
  (`instant_loss_on_wizard_death`, `_on_player_hero_fell` -> `lose(3.0)` = an `after()` timer in mission time). Within
  those 3 s a soldier's hit takes the King to 1 HP -> `_fall()` -> `fire_once(E14)` -> `hold_world(true)` -> `win(0.0)`
  is ignored (an ending is already pending). `MissionBase._physics_process` only adds to `_held_total` while the world is
  held and never runs `_run_timers`, so the defeat never fires and nothing releases the hold: the game sits frozen with
  no banner. Same path from the King's `death_guard`.
- Fix: `if is_ending(): return` at the top of `_on_king_hit`, `_check_fall`, `_fall` (and `_phase2` / `_phase3`, which
  otherwise start a film during a pending defeat).

## 2. High: Warden Gates (and e8m2 pylons) are not part of the save

- `scripts/campaign/mission_e6_base.gd:239` `add_warden_gate` (WardenGate is a StaticBody3D: not in "buildings", not a
  Unit), `scripts/core/save_codec.gd:37-40` (any other node encodes to SKIP, so a mission variable holding gates is
  skipped as a whole), `scripts/core/save_world.gd` (no gate rows). Missions affected: E6m2 (`WGATES`, counter
  `gates_broken` :152), E6m4 (`west_broken` / `east_broken` :124), E8m1 Fire (`wgs`), E8m1 Shadow (`stair`), E8m1
  Nature (`bridge_gates` :54/:334), E8m2 (`pylons` :135, `gates` :140).
- What is wrong: after a load every gate is rebuilt by the start at full HP and open (and spawning tides where it has a
  spawner), whatever its state at the save. E6m2 already restores its FrostGate by hand (`mission_e6_02.gd` load_state)
  but not the Warden Gates.
- Scenarios:
  - E8m1 Nature: `bridge_gates` is skipped, the rebuilt value is `{}`, `_tick_bridges` sees `t >= at` and
    `not bridge_gates.has(id)` for every past bridge and re-creates all of them, including the ones the player broke;
    each spawns a tide every 45 s.
  - E6m2 / E6m4: a gate broken before the save stands again; breaking it again counts twice
    (`gates_broken += 1`), so `sec_gates` can complete with one real gate.
  - E8m2: the player's pylon stands again after a load; the keyhole needs it broken again (`_tick_keyholes` :338).
- Fix: in `MissionE6Base.save_state` / `load_state` write and restore every child `WardenGate` by `gate_id`
  (`hp`, `_dead` -> `_crumble()`, `is_open`, `tides`), and in E8m1 Nature rebuild `bridge_gates` from the restored
  gates (or save the ids of the bridges already raised).

## 3. High: connections and death guards set mid-mission are not restored by a load

- `scripts/campaign/mission_base.gd:1725` `load_state` restores variables, timers by name, guards and waves, but a
  `died.connect(...)` or a `death_guard` set on a unit after the start is not re-made. E5 / E6 missions re-apply theirs
  in `load_state` (`aldra.death_guard = _aldra_guard`...); the E7 / E8 missions do not.
- Cases (all saveable at that moment):
  - `mission_e7_01.gd:713` `_laurel_end`: `seraphine.died.connect(_on_seraphine_died)`. After a load her death no
    longer calls `lose()`: `obj_seraphine` stays active and the mission is still won through the hold (`_final` needs
    only `wins >= 2`). The escort's fail condition is bypassed.
  - `mission_e7_01.gd:759` (home), `mission_e7_03.gd:876` (Seraphine arrives), `mission_e7_04.gd:666` (Dacian and
    Seraphine hold the rock), `mission_e8_03.gd` `_chains_end` (Hush): `guard_life` lost, these story units die for good
    after a load; in E7m4 that weakens the rock defence that decides the defeat.
  - `mission_e7_03.gd:748` `u.died.connect(_on_captain_died)`: kill the Mask Captain after a load and `sec_mask`,
    flag `e7m3_mask` and the unmask film never happen.
  - `mission_e8_01_ice.gd:282` refugees: `lost` stops counting after a load (display and flags only; doom still checked
    on the tick).
- Fix: in each mission's `load_state` (after `super`), re-connect and re-guard the restored refs, e.g. E7m1:
  `if seraphine and has_fired(&"E11") and not seraphine_home: seraphine.died.connect(_on_seraphine_died)` and
  `if seraphine_home: guard_life(seraphine, ...)`; E7m3: `if captain and is_instance_valid(captain): captain.died.connect(...)`.

## 4. Medium: the bot can miss the restored buildings (slot adoption is one-shot)

- `scripts/ai/commander.gd:582` `_setup` -> `_adopt_buildings()` (:659), run once on the first `_think` after
  `match_started`; Commander has no `save_restoring` check (the flag only exists on MissionBase, `mission_base.gd:108`).
  `scripts/core/save_restorer.gd` applies phase 1 on the first `_process` after `match_started`.
- Scenario: in a loaded skirmish the bot's timer (running since `_ready`) fires its first think on the frame
  `match_started` turns true, before the restorer's phase 1. `_adopt_buildings` sees only the rebuilt keep, the saved
  Halls / towers / expansion Hall respawn afterwards and never fill `_slot_building`. The bot then tries H1, S1, X1...
  again at occupied spots (wasted orders and crystals, expansion logic restarts).
- Fix: Commander `_think` returns while `SaveGame.is_loading(_level)`, or SaveWorld.apply_phase2 calls
  `_adopt_buildings()` on every Commander.

## 5. Medium: saved construction sites are rebuilt complete, then reverted by hand

- `scripts/core/save_world.gd:266` uses `spawn_complete` for every saved row; `scripts/buildings/building.gd`
  `load_snapshot` then sets `_progress = p` and removes the pop bonus. `_complete()` (`building.gd:396`) already emitted
  `construction_finished` and `Events.building_completed`.
- Scenario: save with a Hall at 40 %. On load the Hall "completes" (signals fire: stats, mission events listening to
  `building_completed`, Commander), is set back to 40 %, and fires the same signals again when it really finishes.
- Fix: rebuild rows with `progress < 1.0` as sites (a `BuildGrid.spawn_site` / place without completion) and set
  `_progress` on them.

## 6. Medium: Retry after loading a mission save loses the difficulty

- `scripts/core/save_game.gd:186-223` `launch` sets `MenuFlow.difficulty` and the level props but never
  `MenuFlow._mission_path`; `menu_flow.gd:249` re-applies `mission_difficulty` on reload only when `_mission_path`
  matches; `MenuFlow.restart` uses `reload_current_scene` (the scene default is `mission_difficulty := 1`,
  `mission_base.gd:62`).
- Scenario: from a skirmish pause menu, Load an e7m2 save made on Hard. It loads on Hard. Lose, press Retry: the
  mission restarts on Normal.
- Fix: in `launch`, for mode "mission": `MenuFlow._mission_path = info.scene` and
  `MenuFlow.difficulty = int(props.get("mission_difficulty", 1))`.

## 7. Medium: restoring a dead wizard fires its death again and erases its guard

- `scripts/units/wizard.gd:767` `death_guard = Callable()` then `take_damage(...)`.
- What is wrong: the guard the rebuilt start gave the wizard is wiped for good (it is not put back after the kill), and
  the `died` handlers run during the load: e.g. E4m1 / E4m4 `_on_bot_wizard_died` shows "Vessa fell", E5m4
  `_on_vessa_died` runs `fire_once(E11)`; their counters are overwritten later by the level / mission state, the HUD
  messages are not.
- Fix: keep the old guard (`var g := death_guard`), kill, then `death_guard = g`; and have mission `died` handlers return
  while `save_restoring` (or set it on the level for Skirmish too).

## 8. Low: saving is refused often in some fights

- `mission_base.gd:1667` `_save_timer_ok` needs a standard Callable; `bind()` and lambdas fail it.
  `mission_e8_03.gd:729` `after(1.6, _hurl_land.bind(target))` every 10-12 s blocks saving about 15 % of the whole
  finale; `mission_e8_02.gd:401` `after(60.0, _push_home.bind(s))` blocks 60 s per seat; E7m1 / E7m2 / E7m4 ally wizard
  respawns (`after(45/60, func ...)`) block 45-60 s after each death.
- Fix: store the argument in a variable / dictionary and call a named method (`after(1.6, _hurl_land_pending)`),
  or let `save_state` write bound args of standard callables.

## 9. Low: off-camera freeze can show a frozen pose through shadows

## 10. Low: worker safety may stall a construction site with no way out

- `scripts/ai/commander.gd` `_maintain_sites` skips a site while `_node_unsafe(b)`; `_path_camp` blocks it while a
  strong living camp sits within 12 m of the keep -> site path. Only the mid / rich expansion paths feed
  `_blocking_camp_target`, so a camp near any other site (a tower slot, H2 / H3, a V-row house on a layout map) keeps
  that site unbuilt with no creep scheduled for it; its crystals stay paid.
- Fix: skip the camp check for sites (keep only the recent-loss rule), or add the blocked site's camp to
  `_blocking_camp_target`.
