## HIGH

### H1. Starfall hits allied seats (friendly fire in 2v2)
- **Where:** `scripts/units/champion_abilities.gd:2228-2230` (`StarfallStrike._land`)
- **What:** the target filter is `if t == null or int(t) == team: continue`, so only the caster's own seat is skipped.
  Every other team takes the damage, allied seats included (ALLY units and buildings). Every other AoE in the
  codebase uses `is_enemy_of` / `Teams.is_allied_fast` (Projectile, SpellZone, CasterHaze, creature / helper / T3 kits).
- **Scenario:** e2m2 after Ysolde joins (ALLY seat). The player's companion champion has the wizard class at L5 and
  autocasts Starfall on a cluster that the ALLY army is fighting in melee. Ysolde's soldiers and any ALLY tower in
  the 3.5 m radius take the full damage. Truced nodes are hit as well (there is no `in_truce` check).
- **Fix:** `if t == null or Teams.is_allied_fast(int(t), team) or (caster and Unit.in_truce(caster, n)): continue`
  (or call `caster.is_enemy_of(n)` while the caster is valid).

### H2. Champion sense and ability scan treat allied seats as enemies
- **Where:** `scripts/ai/champion_sense.gd:477-480` (units: `if int(u.team) == team` = own, everything else goes into
  `enemies`), `:527` / `:539-552` (allied keeps / towers go into `structures` as danger),
  `scripts/units/champion_abilities.gd:1256-1264` (`_scan`: same split into `_enemies` / `_allies`).
- **What:** after the 2v2 change, "not my seat" no longer means "enemy". `danger_at()` adds the allied army plus
  250 / 150 for each allied keep / tower. `nearest_enemy()` and the `hits` attackers can return allied units.
  `_enemies` feeds every autocast trigger.
- **Scenario (e2m2 / e3m1, companion champion of PLAYER):**
  (a) The champion is hit by a ranged attacker it cannot see. `_rule_fight` falls back to
  `sense.nearest_enemy(me, …)` (champion_brain.gd:722), which can return an adjacent ALLY soldier. The ally becomes
  `_fight_target`, and `_order_attack` (champion_brain.gd:1875) calls `command_attack(ally)` every 0.5 s. Unit
  refuses it because the ally is not an enemy. `_rule_fight` still returns true, so the champion stands still and
  runs no other rule for up to FIGHT_HOLD.
  (b) The champion counts the ALLY base's keep and towers as danger, so it flees from or refuses flags near the
  allied base.
  (c) POINT-trigger abilities aim at clusters that contain allied units (this compounds H1).
- **Fix:** classify with `Teams.is_allied_fast(int(u.team), team)` (allied = own side: skip, or add to `_allies`),
  and skip allied buildings in `structures` the way NEUTRAL ones are skipped.

## MEDIUM

### M1. A 2v2 Commander is blind to the non-lane hostile seat
- **Where:** `scripts/ai/commander.gd:694` (`_scan`: enemy units only from `by_team[_enemy_team]`), `:710`
  (`_enemy_buildings` only `_enemy_team`), `:908` (`_on_building_damaged` only remembers attackers of `_enemy_team`),
  `:3002` (Vessa cast check: foes only `_enemy_team`).
- **What:** `_enemy_team` is the lane opponent (`Teams.opponent_of`). In 2v2 a second seat is also hostile.
  `_rule_defend`, memory, `_nearest_enemy_fighter` (worker flee), retreat strength and the cast threat checks never
  see that seat.
- **Scenario:** e2m2 sets `seat_opponents = {ALLY: SHADOW, ICE: PLAYER, …}`. Ice (ENEMY_2) units walking into the ALLY
  base or hitting its buildings never trigger the ALLY bot's rule 1 defence. Its workers do not flee and its army
  stays at the rally; only individual auto-aggro answers. The ICE Commander likewise ignores ALLY raiders.
- **Fix:** in `_scan`, `_on_building_damaged` and the cast checks, loop over `Teams.hostile_seats(team)` (still
  excluding NEUTRAL). Keep `_enemy_team` only for where to attack.

### M2. Splash damage ignores mission truces
- **Where:** `scripts/units/projectile.gd:111` and `:119` (splash filter uses only `Teams.is_allied_fast`),
  `scripts/spells/spell_zone.gd:93-98` (`_is_enemy` uses only Teams), `champion_abilities.gd:2229` (Starfall).
- **What:** `Unit.is_enemy_of` honours `Unit.in_truce` (MissionBase.set_truce / withdraw_seat `&"all"`), but the area
  damage paths do not. Single-target attacks are safe because their targeting goes through `is_enemy_of`.
- **Scenario:** in a mission with `set_truce` (e2m1 to e3m4 use it), or a seat withdrawn with `withdraw_seat` (truce
  `&"all"`, at peace with everyone), Vessa's Fireball or an Ember Step / Soul Siphon zone cast on a nearby enemy
  blob damages the truced units standing in it. Their `take_damage` fight-back does not answer, because it checks
  `is_enemy_of`, so the truce breaks one way and silently.
- **Fix:** pass the shooter into the filter: `if source valid and not source.is_enemy_of(u): continue`. The same
  goes for SpellZone (via `caster`) and Starfall.

### M3. Pyre Adept Flame Burst is an O(n^2) scan every 0.5 s
- **Where:** `scripts/units/new_units_12.gd:241-247` (`_try_flame_burst`) with `_enemies_near` (`:396-404`)
- **What:** for each enemy within 8 m, it runs another full `get_nodes_in_group("units")` pass with `is_enemy_of`.
  With k candidates and n units, that is (k+1)*n Variant-heavy checks per adept per tick.
- **Scenario:** a late Fire army with 6 Pyre Adepts fighting a 25-unit blob on a 200-unit map: about 6 x 26 x 200 =
  31k `is_enemy_of` calls every 0.5 s, during the fights where the frame budget is already tight. Wave Strike lanes
  make this common.
- **Fix:** take `_enemies_near(pos, range + radius)` once and cluster inside that list (squared distances).

### M4. Caster / champion / helper "ally" spells only see their own seat, unlike wizard spells
- **Where:** `scripts/units/caster_ability.gd:264`, `:797`, `:889`, `:1099`, `:1138`; `champion_abilities.gd:803`,
  `:857`, `:1945`, `:2081`, `:2086`; `helper_abilities.gd:639`; `helper.gd:347`
  (all `int(u.team) != int(owner.team)`).
- **What:** `SpellCaster._friend_team`, `SupportCaster.find_target` and `Unit._refresh_auras` were moved to
  `Teams.is_allied_fast`, but these filters were not. In 2v2, buffs, heals, Blood Tithe and Mending Draught never
  reach an allied seat, and ChampionAbilities `_is_own` treats allied buildings as foreign.
- **Scenario:** e3m1: the ALLY seat's caster or support units next to the player's army never heal or buff the
  player's units, while wizard spells and support casters do.
- **Fix:** decide the rule once (`Teams.is_allied_fast`, or explicitly "own seat only" in a comment) and apply it to
  every ally filter.

Read-only review, 2026-10-08. Every finding was checked against the code. Nothing here is a crash on the normal

## Medium

### M1. Hero WASD: a walk key released during a pause stays "held" (the wizard keeps walking)
- `scripts/ui/wizard_control.gd:33-47` (`_input` keeps `_held`), `:50-53` (cleared only on window focus loss).
- WizardControl is a child of SelectionManager, and neither sets `process_mode` (pausable). While the tree is paused
  (pause menu, briefing, any `get_tree().paused`) the node gets no `_input`, so a key-up is lost.
- Scenario: hold W, press Esc (pause opens), let go of W, Resume: `_held` still has KEY_W and the wizard walks
  until W is tapped again.
- Fix: clear `_held` on NOTIFICATION_PAUSED / UNPAUSED, or check `Input.is_physical_key_pressed` in `_process`.

### M2. PO merge / import never turn an existing singular entry into a plural one (the plural is lost)
- `tools/i18n/i18n_extract.gd:423` (merge) and `:467` (import) call `fr.add(ctx, id, plural)`.
  `tools/i18n/po_file.gd:36-39` returns the existing entry unchanged, so `plural` stays "". (`_add` at
  `i18n_extract.gd:190` fixes this for the template, but the French side is never fixed.)
- Then `po_file.gd:180-186` writes `msgstr` only (strs[0]): an imported `["sing", "plur"]` loses its plural form.
  `coverage()` and `check_french()` (`:551` only looks at the fr entry's own `plural`) still count it as translated.
- Scenario: `Loc.t("{n} worker")` is changed to `Loc.tn("{n} worker", "{n} workers", n)`. After `merge`, the French
  entry is still singular, so `translate_plural` has no plural message: the French shows English (or the singular)
  for n > 1, and test_i18n stays green. Nothing is affected today (all 8 plural keys are plural in fr), so the
  defect is latent.
- Fix: in merge / import, `if e.plural != "" and f.plural == "": f.plural = e.plural; resize strs to 2`.

### M3. The hand-kept French files are loaded by Loc but never checked for placeholders or '#'
- `tools/i18n/i18n_extract.gd:536-553` `check_french()` reads only `AREAS` files. `data/i18n/fr/game_logic_templates.po`
  (70 entries, the Loc.sink '#' templates) and `story_e4_e8.po` (918 entries) are never checked.
