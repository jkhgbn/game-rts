# G-E art-direction review (2026-10-15)

Read / shoot only. 105 windowed shots (1600x900) at High, plus a few at Ultra and Low, on Crown Hollow (4 kingdoms, bases =
keep + Hall + 3 houses + 2 towers + Stables + Forge + every unit type, player and enemy colours, a Shadow mirror), the 4 ready
maps (Crown Hollow, Thornwater, Quartermeet, Ashwind Reach) and 3 missions (m02 Ice, m00 night, m03 Sighing Wood as the
"Nature" one: no Nature-look mission exists, e8m1_nature uses the warm preset too).

- Contact sheet (24 numbered tiles, the numbers below): `docs/art/lab/ge_review/ge_contact_sheet.jpg`
- Key PNGs: `docs/art/lab/ge_review/ge_key_{mirror_lineup,fog_of_war,battle_z30,ice_base,night_mission,overview_pads}.png`
- Every other shot stays local: `docs/art/lab/ge_review/<run>/` (`high_fs` Fire/Shadow, `high_in` Ice/Nature, `high_mirror`,
  `low_is`, `ultra_nf`, `high_maps`, `high_missions`). The folder has a `.gdignore`.
- Tool: `docs/art/lab/ge_review/tools/ge_shots.gd` (modes `bases` / `map` / `mission`, see its header) and `ge_sheet.py`.
  Not judged (still in progress): trees, menu diorama, forges, Fire stables, riders, Nature mounts, Ice T2, selection rings /
  HP bars.

Overall: the game now looks good. Low still looks decent (tiles 20-21: same palette and shapes, flatter light), Ultra
adds little over High at RTS zoom (22-23). Light mood is consistent between the 4 skirmish maps (all the warm preset). The
problems are mostly readability and value balance, plus a few concrete bugs.

Side note for the lead: during the runs, files other agents were editing failed to parse for a while (`IceShapes.put()`,

## Top 8 fixes for maximum beauty per hour

1. **Fog of war `fog_disabled` + a dark cool tint** (item 2, S): one line changes every in-game frame.
2. **Team cue on units** (item 1, M): readability of every fight; mirror matches become playable.
3. **Shadow / Nature / Ice value pass** (item 3, M): balances the 4 kingdoms side by side.
4. **Cap building emissive / bloom** (item 4, S): removes the white discs in every base shot.
5. **`fog_disabled` on additive ground glows + a check rule** (item 5, S-M).
6. **Night mission fill light** (item 6, S): turns the prologue from black to moonlit.
7. **Ice keep halo shards + Fire Hall black quad** (items 7 and 11, S each): the two most visible model bugs.
8. **Ice mission snow value + snowbanks** (item 8, M): the campaign's only snow map.
