# Art audit, whole game (2026-09-26, end of day)

Art director review for the lead. Sources: all 39 Art Lab scorecards in `docs/art/lab/*/scorecard.md` (lab v1.0, runs
21:50-23:11) + `improvement-log.md`, `docs/plan/lead-status.md` (art and Art Lab notes), `docs/plan/art-lab.md`,
`docs/design/art-keep.md`, the art lists in `docs/design/` (map-fill 10, cavalry-mounts 10, tier3-extras 12,
titans-2 11, walls 16, building-levels 11, research 10, grand-market 10, story-episode-1 8), a listing of `art/`, and
a few contact sheets / screens (Ysolde, Sprigling, Crown altar, Fire Keep, map_polish3, 12_v6 riverbank).

**Big picture.** The look is consistent and good: painted shading, warm light, clear kingdom colours. The Shadow and Fire
armies and the landmarks are in good shape (94-98). The weak spots are (1) **Nature reads green on green grass** and
its team colour is tiny at 40 m, (2) **the 4 wizards** are over budget and stick up through their HP bar, (3) **too many
small glow points** on the big objects (Fire Keep, Crown altar), and (4) **most of the game has never been through the
Art Lab**: 39 assets are scored, about 90 are not (all buildings but the Fire Keep, titans, cavalry, champions, beasts,
VFX families, 9 of 10 decor sets).

**Read the FAILs with care.** 10 of the 11 FAIL results are rule B1 "authored meshes" (Ice / Nature soldiers). Their draw
calls pass. The user decided at 23:20 that Art Lab v2 counts meshes **after** the kit merge, so most of these FAILs
should turn green without art work. Do not spend art time on B1 before the v2 re-run. The only real budget FAIL is the
Frostrune Rifleman's vertex count (3 349 > 2 500).

## 2. Area notes

### 4 wizards
- All four WARN (88-92). Same three issues on all: parts above the HP bar line, body_height set too low (head top x1.3-1.47),
  vertices over the soft 3 000 (Ysolde 7 715 is the worst, Vessa 3 795, Malrik 3 632, Torvald 4 596).
- Vessa vs Malrik and Torvald vs Vessa silhouettes are close (IoU 0.68-0.72): hats + robes. One distinct big shape each
  (Vessa crown flames kept low and wide, Malrik a hunched cowl, Torvald a broad fur mantle) would help.
- Ysolde: the only wizard that melts into the grass; see table row 2. Death and cast poses read well.

### Fire army (Cinder Hand 97, Ash Hurler 95, Ember Archer 95, Flame Guard 94; all PASS/WARN)
- Good. Small items: Flame Guard is flat in value (tops +1 L*) with low detail at 25 m; Ash Hurler's ash ball sits above
  the HP bar; Ember Archer / Flame Guard footprints wider than their selection radius (data or pull the bow / shield in).
- Not scored: Cinderlock Musketeer, Blaze Rider.

### Shadow army (Shade 98, Nightbolt 97, Gloom Thrall 96, Shadow Knight 96, Grave Hound 94)
- The best army. Shadow Knight's merged helm / blade reaches 1.3 m above the bar; Grave Hound is busy at 25 m and darker
  on top than below (-3). Otherwise only "Improve next" polish (idle secondary motion).
- Not scored: Barrow Giant, Gloam Rider.

### Ice army (Hauler 94, Seer 94, Shardcaster 91, Colossus 90, Frostrune 90, Rimeshield 89)
- All FAIL on B1 only (except Frostrune: real vertex FAIL). Re-run after Art Lab v2.
- Real readability items: Rimeshield tops too dark, Frostrune / Shardcaster look alike, glow placed low. Contrast is fine
  (blue-white on green).
- Not scored: Rimehorn Rider (+ axeman). Ice light preset and snow ground are not in the lab yet, so Ice has never been
  judged on its own terrain.

### Nature army (Thornling 96, Mossback 93, Sapling 93, Bloomtender 92, Burr Slinger 90, Bramblehorn 89, Sprigling 88)
- Weakest army visually: green on grass, small team colour, heads through the HP bar, blob heavies. See table rows 1 and 11.
- Open user decision: Mossback eyes no longer glow (confirm).
- Not scored: Stagrunner, Glade Outrider.

### Champions (Oathwall, Coin-Cutter, Wayfinder, Mender, Mage Archer, Wildcaller)
- None scored. Wildcaller spirit scenes (13c.V6) are missing. Mage Archer variant B not checked. Champion ability icons
  exist (24); icons for Mage Archer / Wildcaller skills do not.

### Titans + riders + mounts

- Not scored: Cinder Dragon, Nightmare Knight, War Mammoth (+ Howdah), Elder Treant, Barrow Giant; mounts Ember Boar,
  Rimehorn, Grove Stag, Roan Courser; riders Blaze, Gloam, Rimehorn, Stagrunner, Glade Outrider.
- Open: Frost Wyrm was built **wingless** but the spec says 8.5 m wings: user must choose. Rimehorn seat sits behind the
