# G-F art-direction review, round 2 (2026-10-15)

Read / shoot only. About 110 windowed shots (1600x900) at High plus an Ultra set, taken after the round-1 fixes
(`docs/art/ge_review.md`, all 14 done): Crown Hollow bases for the 4 kingdoms (keep + Hall + 3 houses + 2 towers + Stables
+ Forge + every unit type), a Shadow mirror, a 4-kingdom battle, the 4 ready maps (Crown Hollow, Thornwater, Quartermeet,
Ashwind Reach / Wave Strike), m00 night, m02 Ice, m03 Sighing Wood, the main-menu diorama, the 4 champions and the 5 titans.

- Contact sheet (24 numbered tiles, the numbers below): `docs/art/lab/gf_review2/gf2_contact_sheet.jpg`
- Key PNGs: `docs/art/lab/gf_review2/gf2_key_{battle_hp_bars,fire_start_ingame,autumn_quartermeet,shadow_mirror_base}.png`
- Every other shot stays local in `docs/art/lab/gf_review2/<run>/` (`high_fs`, `high_in`, `high_mirror`, `high_maps`,
  `high_missions`, `high_close`, `ultra_nf`, `menu`). The folder has a `.gdignore`.
- Tools: `docs/art/lab/gf_review2/tools/gf2_shots.gd` (round 1's `ge_shots.gd` plus a `closeups` mode: champions + titans
  on the Crown Hollow meadow) and `gf2_sheet.py`. Menu: `art/environment/menu_diorama/menu_diorama_shots.gd`
  `out=res://docs/art/lab/gf_review2/menu`.

Overall: a clear step up. Every frame now reads as one game. What is left is mostly UI noise in fights (HP bars), the
autumn maps' palette, a few flat or placeholder-looking models at close zoom, and loud ground rings and particles.
Ultra adds little over High at RTS zoom (tiles 23-24 vs 3 and 8), as in round 1.

## What clearly improved since round 1
- Fog of war: dark, cool and calm (tile 9). It was a beige wash.
- Team readability: the player's units have green rings and the enemy's red ones. In a mirror match the enemy wears the
  teal alt palette on units and buildings, so the two sides read at a glance (tiles 4-5, `high_mirror/base_shadow_e_z30`).
- Kingdom balance: Nature units are cream / bark and stand off the grass; Ice snow and shards are toned down and the
  paper halo is gone; the enemy Shadow base is slate with violet accents (tiles 2, 3, 24).
- Bloom: there are almost no white discs left on keeps and halls (one exception, item 4).
- Overviews: no square light pads and no distance haze on the green maps (tiles 13-14).
- Missions: m00 is moonlit blue and playable (18). The m02 lake is dark cracked ice and the snow is no longer glare (17).
  m03 is a darker wood with an Oldroot centrepiece (19).
- Neutral thatch is muted, Nature death leaves are smaller, and the Ashwind lane has banners and rocks.

## Top 8 fixes for beauty per hour

1. **Calmer ground rings** (item 5, S): rim only, less alpha, slower pulse. It cleans up every Nature base and fight and the mammoth.
2. **Lighter HP bars** (item 1, M): thinner, sized by unit, fade after the hit. Every fight shows the models again.
3. **Autumn palette + fog** (item 2, S-M): olive grass vs red-brown dirt and lighter fog. Two of the 4 ready maps, including Wave Strike's only map.
4. **Cap the white particle blobs** (item 7, S): Ice snow motes and Nature puffs.
5. **Fire keep crown -25 % + crucible emissive cap** (item 4, S): the first frame of most Fire matches and missions.
6. **Shadow roofs + player keep saturation** (item 3, M): the last flat models at close zoom, and a calmer Shadow side in mirrors.
7. **Shadow site pad + m02 ice blocks** (items 8 and 11, S each): the two "placeholder" shapes still on screen.
8. **Ice Hall roof** (item 6, M): the brightest flat square in the Ice base.
